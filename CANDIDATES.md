# Quiet dread review candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: a code review comment
that approves the pull request while radiating quiet
existential dread.

1. None of us will be here: "LGTM. The code works, the tests
   pass, and none of us will be here to see what it becomes."
   -- the dread is in the timescale; warm and correct on the
   surface.
2. Outlive us: "Approved. Everything passes, nothing matters,
   and this function will outlive us all. Ship it." -- three
   flat clauses; the approval is real, the hope is not.
3. Future archaeologist: "Merging. Someday someone will dig
   here and wonder what we were thinking. We were thinking
   about lunch." -- funny, the dread is lighter.
4. Temporary forever: "Approved. It's a temporary fix, like
   everything else that has ever shipped, including us." --
   the "including us" turn is strong.
5. Green check: "All checks green. The void has been notified
   and has no further comments." -- elegant, maybe too poetic
   for a review thread.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 4. Candidates 1 and 2 keep the dread at a
distance -- the code will outlive us, someday, in general.
Candidate 4 makes it this comment, this merge, this team: a
temporary fix, like everything that has ever shipped,
"including us". The approval is genuine and the ground under
it is gone. That is quiet dread at its quietest.
